Pace, FL

Pest control for Pace homes and businesses

Pace is a fast-growing community in Santa Rosa County, just north of Escambia Bay and northeast of Pensacola. It grew from about 20,100 people in 2010 to about 24,700 in 2020, and it is the second-largest community in the county. Floridatown and Pea Ridge are part of the area.

New subdivisions built on cleared land often see fire ants, field mice and termites right away. Wooded lots bring ticks, spiders and wildlife. Homes near Escambia Bay deal with mosquitoes and moisture.
