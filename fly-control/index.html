Overview

Fly Control for Pensacola Homes

A few flies in summer are normal. A steady stream of flies inside, day after day, means they are breeding somewhere close. That could be a dirty drain, a garbage can, a pet area or a hidden piece of rotting food.

Fly control is mostly detective work. A technician identifies the fly, finds the breeding source and helps remove it. Treatments, baits and traps then knock down the adults.

Local Know-How

Flies in Pensacola

Pensacola's heat speeds up the fly life cycle. In summer, house flies can go from egg to adult in about a week. Trash, pet waste and compost in hot weather breed flies fast.

Drain flies are common in older homes with slow drains and in bathrooms that are not used often. A sudden burst of large flies can mean a rat or other animal died in the attic or a wall.

Good to Know

Risks and preparation

What happens if you wait

House flies land on garbage and waste, then on food and counters. They can spread germs that cause stomach illness. Heavy fly activity can also signal a hidden problem like a dead animal or leak.

How to get ready for your visit

  • Take out trash and clean the cans
  • Run water in unused drains
  • Remove overripe fruit
  • Note when and where flies show up most
Prevention

How to keep pests from coming back

  • Keep trash cans closed and clean
  • Clean drains weekly with an enzyme cleaner
  • Pick up pet waste daily
  • Store produce in the fridge in summer
  • Repair torn window screens

FAQ

Fly Control FAQ

Why do I have flies in my house?
Flies are breeding nearby, often in a drain, trash can, pet area or rotting food.
How do I get rid of drain flies?
Clean the drain to remove the slime layer where they breed. Enzyme cleaners work well.
Do bug zappers help?
They kill some flies but do not stop breeding. Finding the source is more important.
Can flies mean there is a dead animal?
Yes. A sudden swarm of large flies can mean a dead rodent in the walls or attic.
Do you help restaurants with flies?
Yes. See commercial fly control for kitchens and food businesses.
