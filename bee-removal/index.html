Overview

Bee Removal for Pensacola Homes

A ball of bees hanging on a branch is usually a swarm. Swarms are looking for a new home and often move on within a day or two. A colony that has moved into a wall, soffit, shed or tree has already built comb and stored honey.

Bee removal depends on which you have. A swarm can often be collected and taken to a beekeeper. An established hive in a wall needs a cutout to remove the bees, comb and honey. Leaving comb behind can lead to melting honey, odors and new pests.

Local Know-How

Bees in Pensacola

Honey bees swarm most in spring, and Pensacola's mild climate gives them a long season. Hollow trees, empty sheds, porch columns and soffits are common nesting spots.

Florida also has Africanized honey bees in parts of the state. They look like normal honey bees but defend their nests much more strongly. Because you cannot tell them apart by sight, any colony near people should be handled by a pro.

Good to Know

Risks and preparation

What happens if you wait

Bee stings hurt and can cause serious reactions in allergic people. A disturbed colony can sting many times. Honey and wax left in walls can attract ants, roaches and new bees.

How to get ready for your visit

  • Keep people and pets away
  • Do not spray, block or seal the entry hole
  • Note where the bees go in and out
  • Tell the technician about allergies in the home
Prevention

How to keep pests from coming back

  • Seal gaps in soffits, siding and eaves
  • Cover water meter and irrigation boxes
  • Fill hollow fence posts and columns
  • Remove old hives and comb fully

FAQ

Bee Removal FAQ

Will a bee swarm leave on its own?
Often within a day or two. If it stays longer, it may be building a hive nearby.
Can you save the bees?
When it is safe, yes. Swarms and many hives can be relocated to a beekeeper.
Why can't you just spray the hive in my wall?
Dead bees and honey left in the wall rot, melt and attract other pests. The comb needs to come out.
How do I know if bees are Africanized?
You cannot tell by looking. Very defensive behavior is a warning sign. Keep away and call a pro.
Is bee removal different from wasp removal?
Yes. Bees are pollinators and are relocated when possible. See wasp and hornet removal for wasps.
